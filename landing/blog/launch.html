Aura Landing Page is Live

A fair social reputation network where points are earned through real-world actions, not manufactured online personas. The landing page is live — the product is coming soon.

Today we're launching the Aura landing page at sergio-pb.github.io/aura-workspace (soon to be ifarm.club once DNS is configured) — a social reputation network built on a simple idea: the people who were actually there should decide who deserves credit.

The Problem

Social media today optimizes for engagement, not truth. Algorithms amplify outrage. Bots inflate reputations. Your online persona has little to do with who you actually are.

Aura flips this. Your reputation is earned through real-world actions, recorded by people who witnessed them, and validated by community consensus. No algorithms. No bots. Just people recognizing people.

How It Works

Aura has two products that work together:

The Farm

A local media studio that captures moments with rich telemetry — location, time, device signatures, face detection. When you see someone do something notable, you record it and attribute "aura points" to them. The Farm works offline — no network required to capture and attribute.

The Card

Your identity beacon and reputation feed. When someone attributes points to you, you claim them through The Card. Your feed shows your earned reputation — and lets others react, confirm, or challenge attributions. Your reputation is portable, verifiable, and yours.

Built Different

  • Local-first. Everything works offline. Network is additive, not required.
  • Privacy-by-design. Telemetry processed on-device. You control what's shared.
  • Transparent scoring. Every point attribution is public and challengeable. No black boxes.
  • Community-governed. Validation by consensus, not central authority.
  • Agent-built. The entire product was built by an autonomous AI agent operating on a 30-minute cadence, with every decision documented and every line of code committed in the open.

What's Next

We're starting with a founding community — a trusted circle of early users who will shape the network's norms. If you're interested in being part of the founding cohort, reach out.

This is just the beginning. Multi-device event correlation, enhanced telemetry, and community validation are already built. Public beta, mobile apps, and network effects are on the roadmap.

Join Us
