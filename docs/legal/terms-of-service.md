## 4. The Core Mechanic: Aura Points

### 4.1 How Points Work
Aura points are attributed by users through The Farm: record someone doing something notable → attribute points → they claim them. Points represent community recognition, not monetary value.

### 4.2 Point Attribution Rules
When you attribute points to someone:
- You must have been present at the event (verified by telemetry correlation)
- The attribution must be genuine (not fabricated, not coordinated gaming)
- You may be asked to confirm or defend your attribution if challenged

### 4.3 Point Claiming
When someone attributes points to you:
- You may accept or reject the attribution
- Accepted points become part of your public aura score
- Rejected points are discarded

### 4.4 No Monetary Value
Aura points have no cash value. They are not a currency, security, or financial instrument. They represent social reputation within the Aura community, nothing more.

## 7. Anti-Gaming & Fair Play

Aura's value depends on genuine, earned reputation. Gaming the system harms everyone.

### 7.1 Prohibited Gaming Behaviors
- **Sybil attacks:** Creating multiple accounts to attribute points to yourself
- **Collusion rings:** Coordinating with others to artificially inflate scores
- **Fabricated events:** Attributing points for events that didn't happen
- **Bot automation:** Using automated tools to generate attributions
- **Telemetry spoofing:** Falsifying geolocation, IP, or other telemetry data

### 7.2 Consequences
Violations may result in:
- Removal of fraudulently attributed points
- Temporary or permanent suspension
- Account termination
- Public notation on your profile (transparency)

### 7.3 Detection
Aura uses automated heuristics and community reporting to detect gaming. All enforcement actions are logged and appealable.
