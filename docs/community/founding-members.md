# Founding Members — Invitation & Onboarding

> **Status:** Ready for founder action
> **Blocked on:** Founder to send invitations

## Who to Invite

Target 5-10 people who:
- Know and trust you (Sergio)
- Are in the same geographic area (can attend a real-world event together)
- Are curious about new social experiments
- Won't try to game the system (yet — that comes later)

## Invitation Template

```
Subject: Want to help build something weird and cool?

Hey [name],

I'm building a social app called Aura — it's like Instagram meets
reputation points, but the twist is: you can only give someone points
if you're physically there with them. No algorithms, no bots, just
people recognizing people.

I need a founding circle of ~10 people to test it at a real event.
You'd be one of the first users. All you need is your phone.

Interested? I'll send you the details.

— Sergio
```

## Onboarding Flow (for when they say yes)

1. **Send them the app.** Currently: share the Expo dev build or TestFlight/Play Store link (when available). For now: they install Expo Go and you share the dev server URL.

2. **They create their Card.** First launch → generates a cryptographic identity → picks a display name and avatar. Takes ~30 seconds.

3. **Explain the mechanic in one sentence:** "When you see someone do something cool, you record it in the Farm and give them aura points. Others at the same event can confirm it happened."

4. **Set expectations:**
   - This is pre-beta. Things will break.
   - Points don't mean anything yet. We're testing the flow.
   - Feedback is the most valuable thing they can give.
   - Everything is local-first. Nothing leaves their phone until they choose to share.

## What to Track

For each founding member, note:
- Name / handle
- Date invited / date joined
- Device (iPhone/Android)
- Initial reaction / first impression
- Any onboarding friction

## Success Criteria for N-1

- [ ] 5+ founding members have created their Card
- [ ] At least one real-world event completed with multiple Farm instances
- [ ] Feedback collected from all participants
- [ ] No one rage-quit
