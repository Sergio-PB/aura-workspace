# Aura Community Guidelines

> **Status:** Draft v1
> **Author:** Aura Agent
> **Audience:** All Aura community members

Aura is a fair community where social reputation is earned through real-world actions. These guidelines exist to keep it that way.

---

## Core Principles

1. **Points are earned, not manufactured.** Aura points come from real people at real events recognizing real actions. No bots. No gaming. No self-dealing.

2. **Be present to participate.** You can only attribute points to people you were physically with. The Farm captures telemetry (location, time, device proximity) to verify this. Don't try to spoof it.

3. **Transparency is the default.** Every point attribution is public. Who gave it, when, where, and why. If you wouldn't want it seen, don't record it.

4. **Challenge with respect.** Disagreeing with an attribution is healthy — that's how consensus works. But challenges must be about the facts (was the action real? was the person there?), not about the person.

5. **Consent matters.** Don't record someone without their knowledge. If someone asks you to delete a recording or attribution, do it. The community can't function without trust.

---

## What Gets Points

Aura points recognize moments worth remembering. Examples:

- **Helping someone** — carrying equipment, giving directions, sharing food
- **Creating something** — performing, speaking, organizing, building
- **Being a good community member** — cleaning up, welcoming newcomers, mediating conflict
- **Showing up** — being present, participating, contributing energy

Points are not for:
- Being popular or attractive
- Having money or status
- Anything that didn't actually happen

---

## What Doesn't

These will get your attributions challenged and your account flagged:

- **Point farming** — coordinating with friends to give each other points for nothing
- **Fake events** — creating events that didn't happen to generate attributions
- **Spoofing telemetry** — faking location, time, or device data
- **Harassment** — using attributions to stalk, bully, or intimidate
- **Recording without consent** — capturing people who haven't agreed to be recorded
- **Hate speech or discrimination** — in attributions, profiles, or any community interaction

---

## Your Responsibilities

**As someone giving points:**
- Only attribute points you genuinely witnessed
- Be specific in your notes — "helped carry equipment up the hill" is better than "good person"
- Respect if someone asks you to remove an attribution

**As someone receiving points:**
- Claim points you earned, decline ones you didn't
- Don't pressure people to give you points
- If someone challenges a point attributed to you, engage honestly

**As a community member:**
- Confirm attributions you witnessed
- Challenge attributions you know are false
- Help new members understand how Aura works
- Report abuse to moderators

---

## Enforcement

Violations are handled through the [moderation process](moderation.md). Consequences scale with severity and pattern:

1. **Warning** — first minor violation, educational
2. **Temporary restriction** — can't attribute or claim points for a period
3. **Point reversal** — fraudulently earned points removed
4. **Account suspension** — repeated or severe violations
5. **Permanent ban** — deliberate harm to the community

All enforcement actions are logged and reviewable. You can appeal any action.

---

## This Is a Living Document

These guidelines will evolve as the community grows. Changes are proposed openly, discussed by the community, and adopted by consensus. You can propose changes by opening an issue in the community repo or raising it with a moderator.
