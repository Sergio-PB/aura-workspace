## Content Pillars

### 1. Product Demos (40%)
Show The Farm and The Card in action. Real moments, real people.
- "Watch someone get aura points for [thing they did]"
- Before/after: "This moment → this reputation"
- Feature spotlights: face detection, event correlation, validation

### 2. Community Moments (30%)
User-generated content. Real events, real attributions.
- Repost user-submitted aura point moments
- Event highlights: "Last night's gathering, ranked by aura"
- Member spotlights: "Most confirmed points this week"

### 3. Behind the Scenes (20%)
Build in public. Transparency is our brand.
- Architecture decisions explained simply
- Agent-built: "Our AI agent shipped this feature"
- Roadmap updates, what we're working on

### 4. Values & Culture (10%)
What we stand for. Why Aura exists.
- "No algorithms. No bots. Just people recognizing people."
- Privacy-first design choices
- Community governance in action

## Launch Sequence

### Phase 0: Pre-Launch (Now)
- Claim all handles (use personal email until domain email exists)
- Set up profiles with logo, bio, link to `ifarm.club`
- Zero posts. Just reserve the namespace.

### Phase 1: Teaser (2 weeks before public launch)
- 3-5 posts per platform
- "Something's coming" tone
- Show product UI screenshots (no real user data)
- Link to waitlist/email signup

### Phase 2: Launch Week (Public launch)
- Daily posts across all platforms
- Founder thread: "Why I built Aura"
- First real event coverage
- Press kit available

### Phase 3: Sustain (Ongoing)
- 3-5 Instagram posts/week
- 1-2 Twitter threads/week
- 2-3 TikToks/week
- Weekly Discord events (office hours, feedback sessions)

## Voice & Tone

- **Casual, not corporate.** We're a club, not a SaaS.
- **Transparent, not mysterious.** Show the work.
- **Optimistic, not cynical.** Social media can be good. We're proving it.
- **Self-aware.** We know "aura points" sounds silly. Lean in.
